# Common 3D Print Lab Workflows
Author: Mo Al-Khasawneh (alkhasmr)

## 1. Overview
Many university 3D print labs follow similar high-level workflows when handling print requests.  
These observations are based on general practices and may differ from UC’s specific process.

## 2. Typical Workflow
1. User prepares a 3D model
2. User submits request or email
3. Staff reviews printability
4. Job is assigned to a printer
5. Print completes or fails
6. User picks up finished part

## 3. Pain Points in Typical Labs
- No central tracking system
- Dependence on SD cards or email
- Difficulty managing multiple printers
- Limited visibility for users

## 4. How EON-OpenSlicer Can Help
- Central dashboard
- Job history tracking
- Clear status visibility
- Reduced manual communication
